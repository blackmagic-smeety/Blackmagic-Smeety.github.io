Fire in Your Eyes

Fire in Your Eyes is an original song written by independent songwriter Blackmagic Smeety. Blending modern R&B, electro-pop and atmospheric synth-pop, the song captures summer nights, attraction, movement and the instant when one look changes the whole atmosphere.

The lyrics focus on physical closeness, dancing, late-night roads and the restless energy of attraction. Blackmagic Smeety wrote the song and used AI and Suno as creative production tools to turn the songwriting into a finished musical experience.
