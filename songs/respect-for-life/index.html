THE SONG

Respect for Life

Respect for Life is an original cinematic pop-rock song written by independent songwriter Blackmagic Smeety. The power ballad reflects on time, growing older, looking back and discovering how much every remaining moment can mean.

Rather than offering a simple motivational slogan, the song asks what we would do differently if we had understood life earlier. It is the English version of the bilingual project Respekt vor dem Leben / Respect for Life. AI and Suno were used as creative production tools for the musical realization.
