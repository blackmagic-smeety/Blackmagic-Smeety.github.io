THE SONG

What Else Is Waiting, Inside of Me

What Else Is Waiting, Inside of Me is an original soft-rock ballad written by independent songwriter Blackmagic Smeety. The song is about self-discovery, moving beyond fear and asking what possibilities may still be waiting inside us.

Its central idea is not about becoming somebody else, but about discovering parts of yourself that have not yet had their chance. Blackmagic Smeety wrote the lyrics and concept; AI and Suno were used as creative production tools for the musical realization.
