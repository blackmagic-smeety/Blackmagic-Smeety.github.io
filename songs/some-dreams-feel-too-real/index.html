THE SONG

Some Dreams Feel Too Real

Some Dreams Feel Too Real is an original song by independent songwriter Blackmagic Smeety. Its cinematic dark-pop and dark-synthpop atmosphere explores dreams that feel so vivid that desire, memory and reality begin to blur.

The song is built around an intimate question: what remains when a dream feels emotionally real after waking? Blackmagic Smeety wrote the lyrics and shaped the concept, with AI and Suno used as creative tools for the musical production.
